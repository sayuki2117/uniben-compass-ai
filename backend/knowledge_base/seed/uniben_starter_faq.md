University of Benin (UNIBEN) - General Information
1. History
Overview
The University of Benin (UNIBEN) is a Federal Government-owned university located in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. It is one of Nigeria's leading public universities and is widely recognized for excellence in teaching, research, innovation, and community service.
UNIBEN was established on 23 November 1970 by the military administration of the then Mid-Western State under Colonel Samuel Osaigbovo Ogbemudia. It was initially founded as the Midwest Institute of Technology.
On 1 July 1971, following approval by the National Universities Commission (NUC), the institution became a full-fledged university and was renamed the University of Benin.
On 1 April 1975, ownership of the University was transferred to the Federal Government of Nigeria, making it one of the country's federal universities. 
Growth and Development
The University began academic activities with only 109 students at the former Teachers' Training College on Ekehuan Road, now known as the Ekehuan Campus.
Over the decades, UNIBEN has grown significantly into a comprehensive university with tens of thousands of undergraduate and postgraduate students, numerous academic programmes, research institutes, and internationally recognized centres of excellence. The university has also served as an academic mentor to several affiliate institutions across Nigeria. 
Achievements
Some notable achievements include:
•	First Nigerian university to develop indigenous software for Computer-Based Testing (CBT). 
•	First Nigerian university to award a PhD in Optometry. 
•	First university in Nigeria to award degrees in Industrial Chemistry. 
•	Ranked among the leading universities in West Africa in Webometrics rankings. 
•	Hosts several internationally funded Centres of Excellence supported by organizations including the World Bank, TETFund, and Shell Petroleum Development Company.

2. **Undergraduate Guide** Undergraduate Guide
Introduction
The University of Benin (UNIBEN) offers undergraduate degree programmes across its colleges, faculties, schools, and institutes. Admission is regulated by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) and the University's admission policies. Students admitted into undergraduate programmes are expected to complete all admission, clearance, registration, and academic requirements before commencing their studies. 
________________________________________
1. Admission Requirements
Undergraduate Admission
Admission into undergraduate programmes is primarily through the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) conducted by JAMB.
General Entry Requirements
Applicants must:
•	Select the University of Benin as a preferred institution through JAMB. 
•	Meet the University's approved UTME eligibility requirements for the admission year. 
•	Possess at least five (5) O'Level credit passes, including English Language and Mathematics, obtained in not more than two sittings. 
•	Meet the subject-specific requirements for the chosen programme. 
Medicine and Surgery
Candidates seeking admission into Medicine and Surgery must obtain the required O'Level credits in one sitting, including the subjects prescribed for the programme. 
________________________________________
Direct Entry Admission
Candidates applying through Direct Entry must satisfy the O'Level requirements and possess one of the following qualifications where applicable:
•	JUPEB 
•	NCE 
•	OND 
•	HND 
•	GCE Advanced Level 
•	Other qualifications recognised by the University 
Admission into Direct Entry programmes is subject to departmental requirements and available spaces. 
________________________________________
Important Notes
•	Admission is merit-based and follows Federal Government admission guidelines. 
•	Candidates may be offered an alternative programme if admission into their preferred programme is not possible. 
•	Candidates with inconsistent or fraudulent admission documents are liable to have their admission withdrawn.

Eligible candidates are invited to participate in the University's Post-UTME (PUTME) screening exercise.
The screening process generally includes:
•	Online application 
•	Verification of candidate information

•	Screening according to the University's published procedures for the admission cycle 
Admission decisions are based on the University's approved selection criteria and departmental requirement

Academic Programme
Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.)
The Faculty offers the Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.), an undergraduate professional degree that provides comprehensive legal education.
Programme Objectives
The programme prepares students to:
•	Understand the Nigerian legal system. 
•	Interpret constitutional and statutory provisions. 
•	Conduct legal research. 
•	Draft legal documents. 
•	Analyze judicial decisions. 
•	Resolve legal disputes using appropriate legal principles.

Programme Duration
•	5 academic years for UTME entrants.
